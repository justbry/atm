## What we use

`packages/balance-engine` (`@autumn/balance-engine`): Autumn's balance rules as pure functions (`compute*`: subject + command in, decision or mutation out). It imports 38 names from `@autumn/shared`; a static, file-level import walk of those reaches 1,208 of `shared`'s 1,292 files (about 74,000 lines), with `zod`, `decimal.js`, `drizzle-orm`, `stripe`, `date-fns`, `better-auth` and `@orpc/*` among the packages they import. Extracting the engine alone is therefore not a copy of a few files: `atm` stays inside this workspace, and a bundled build (`bun build`) is what tree-shakes it down. `bun run --cwd packages/atm build` gives one 1.99 MB file, `dist/atm.js`, with the engine and only what it reaches.
