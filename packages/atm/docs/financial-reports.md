# Financial reports from the log

What exists, and what is still missing, for hledger journals, reports and a reconciliation made from the log. Moved here from `ROADMAP.md` in the roadmap split; the wording is the original. The built items (#46, #48, #49) are also in `CHANGELOG.md` (Unreleased); `ROADMAP.md` keeps each open item as one line. Related: `docs/log-and-r2.md` (the shipper, R2, the manifest), `docs/receipts.md` (tax lines), `docs/releasing.md`, `docs/README.md`.

## What exists

The pieces that exist on `dev` (not released): the handler writes decision and money events to the outbox (#49), the family service can write its own commands there (#46), `atm ship` puts them in the folder (#41), `atm journal` makes hledger journals, `atm report` prints the reports (#47; checked on the golden year, #42), `atm reconcile` compares them with Stripe (#48). Not yet true end to end, found by reading the code on `dev` at `acee2e6d5`:

## The money lines (#49)

- **The handler's log had no money lines at all; now it has them** (#49, `cbe774ffd`, on `dev`, not released). Before it, on `dev` at `acee2e6d5`, the handler wrote only `credit.*` and `plan.changed`; the postings for payments, refunds and payouts existed and only the golden year's generator used them, so the golden-year reports (2,138.54 USD in `assets:clearing:stripe-payouts`, 71.46 of fees) showed more than a handler-made log held. (An earlier version of this item said cash stayed in `assets:stripe` at the gross amount; that was wrong.) Now `invoice.paid` makes a `payment.succeeded`, a `charge.refunded` of a handled invoice a `payment.refunded`, and `payout.paid` (a new mapping) a `payout.paid`, checked end to end with the real handler, `atm ship`, `journalMonth` and hledger (260.00 income, 50.00 refunds, 7.84 fees, 202.16 in the payout clearing account). The review of #49 fixed an upgrade invoice booking its income to the old tier and a refund booking against a payment that was never booked.
  - [ ] The fee is the caller's: the family service passes `paymentFee` (it reads the charge's balance transaction before calling the handler, and answers 5xx when that lookup fails, so Stripe retries). Without it the payment books gross and says `fee_known: false`. A fee that arrives later is not booked.
  - [ ] Not built: a reversal of a payout that fails after `payout.paid`, non-USD books (a non-USD invoice, refund, payout or dispute is answered 200 `non_usd` with a `money.skipped` marker and no money line, credits still granted: see "Non-USD" below), tax or customer-balance effects separated from the amount paid, and a `charge.refunded` without `previous_attributes.amount_refunded` books the whole running total. Real Stripe behaviour (`previous_attributes`, upgrade-invoice line order, Connect payout events) was not checked.

## Disputes and Stripe's dispute fee

- **What was booked before this change.** The reference handler booked no money line for a dispute at all (`charge.dispute.created` and `.closed` changed credits only: a lost dispute took the unused credits back, an open or won one changed nothing). The postings for `payment.disputed` and `dispute.closed` existed (`moneyPostings`: the disputed amount out of `assets:stripe` into `expenses:billing:disputes`, and back when won, nothing when lost) and only the golden-year generator wrote them. Income was never reversed by a dispute, a lost one leaves the income and the disputes expense, and the fee was in neither.
- **What the payload carries.** The fee is in the event, so it is not a caller input (unlike `paymentFee`, which the invoice event cannot carry). A `charge.dispute.created` and a `charge.dispute.closed` carry the dispute's own `balance_transactions`; the first is the withdrawal. In the recorded test-mode events (`fixtures/billing/stripe/recorded/delivered/dispute-lost` and `dispute-won`; API `2025-10-29.clover`; see that folder's README for how they were captured): `amount` -26000, `fee` 1500 (`fee_details`: type `stripe_fee`, description "Dispute fee"), `net` -27500, type `adjustment`, reporting category `dispute`. An inquiry (`warning_needs_response`, `recorded/charge.dispute.created.json`) has an empty list: Stripe took nothing. The mapping reads `balance_transactions[0].amount` as `withdrawn` and `.fee` as `fee`; a dispute with no withdrawal books no line.
- **What is booked now.** On `charge.dispute.created` (and on `charge.dispute.closed`, see below), when money moved: one `payment.disputed` with `assets:stripe` -(amount + fee), `expenses:billing:disputes` +amount and `expenses:billing:dispute-fees` +fee (a separate account, so the cost of disputes beyond the disputed sum is visible; the lines sum to zero). For the recorded dispute: -275.00, +260.00, +15.00. When it closes, `dispute.closed`: lost has no postings (the opening stands: the income stays, 260.00 is a loss and so is the 15.00); won has `assets:stripe` +amount and `expenses:billing:disputes` -amount, so only the fee is lost.
- **The fee is not given back when the dispute is won.** In the recorded won dispute the closing event lists a second balance transaction, "Chargeback reversal", amount +26000, fee 0, and nothing returns the 1500. That is one test-mode recording; Stripe's own pricing pages may say otherwise for some accounts or countries, and nothing here read them. If Stripe ever returns the fee it would arrive as a balance transaction with a negative `fee` that this change does not book, and `reconcile` would show it as a fees difference.
- **Redelivery and order.** `payment.disputed` has the idem `stripe:<dispute id>` and `dispute.closed` `stripe:<dispute id>:closed`, so a re-send under another event id, or the same dispute reaching the handler through both events, books each once. A `charge.dispute.closed` carries the same withdrawal as the opening, so it also makes the `payment.disputed` (dated at the withdrawal's own balance transaction, `balance_transactions[0].created`, which both events carry: the same instant either way, and the one `reconcile` scopes Stripe's fee and net by. It can be a second after the dispute's `created`, so a dispute opened in the last second of a month has its cash in the next one; a won dispute's `dispute.closed` is dated by the reversal, `balance_transactions[1].created`, the same way): whichever arrives first books it, the other is dropped by the outbox. That covers a created event that never came, or one that arrives after the closing.
- **`reconcile`** compares Stripe's fees with the postings to both `expenses:billing:stripe-fees` and `expenses:billing:dispute-fees`, and the net with `assets:stripe`, which now holds the withdrawal with its fee, so a handler-made log with a lost and a won dispute reconciles clean (`tests/handler-disputes.test.ts`). A log written before this change has no fee on its disputes and shows it as `amount_differs` fees and net, as before.
- **Not built.** `charge.dispute.updated` and `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn` / `funds_reinstated` are not mapped (an inquiry that later becomes a chargeback is booked when it closes, dated by the withdrawal's balance transaction, so in the month the funds left); a closing status other than `won` or `lost` (`warning_closed`, `prevented`, `charge_refunded`) books nothing; a dispute in another currency books nothing and leaves a `money.skipped` marker (see "Non-USD"); the dispute `amount` is read as the withdrawal, which holds when the dispute and the balance are in the same currency. The `balance_transaction` definition in `schema/stripe/objects.clover.json` (the shape check for the dispute's list) was written by hand from the recorded payload because Stripe's full spec was not at hand; `bun run stripe-spec <spec3.json>` (`balance_transaction` is now kept by `schema/stripe/build.ts`) replaces it, and that run was not made.

## Non-USD

- **Before.** A non-USD invoice, refund or payout made no money line (the postings are USD only), and the delivery was answered 200 `applied` as for any other: nothing in the response, the outbox or the logs said a line had been left out. The invoice's credits were granted.
- **Now: keep granting the credits, answer 200, and say so.** The delivery is answered 200 with the outcome `non_usd` (`body.skipped` lists `<kind> <Stripe id> <amount> <currency>`; `body.events` and `body.wroteRows` are as for `applied`). Each line left out writes one `money.skipped` event to the outbox (no postings; payload `id`, `kind` invoice, refund, payout or dispute, `reason` `non_usd`, `currency`, `amount` in minor units; idem `stripe:<id>:non_usd`, so a re-send leaves one). The claim row in `atm_event_claims` keeps the outcome `non_usd`. `deps.warn` (default: a line on stderr) is called once, after the commit; a `warn` that throws is caught and the line goes to stderr, so the 200 stands. `reconcile` counts the markers dated in the period in `result.skipped` (`{ non_usd: n }`) and prints "skipped in the log"; `atm report` prints a count on stderr, so the hledger output on stdout is not changed.
- **Why not refuse.** A non-2xx makes Stripe retry for up to three days, then email the account and possibly disable the endpoint; the currency of an invoice cannot change, so no retry could succeed, and a refusal (422, claim released, nothing written) would hold back the credits of a family that has paid. The menu names its own currency (`menu.currency`, and lookup keys carry it), so a school with a non-USD menu would be refused on every invoice. The credits are the product; the USD books are the report. The only price of 200 is that the books are short, which is why the answer is distinct and counted instead of silent. The existing refusal for a paid add-on nothing would grant (`addon_only_subscription`) is the other way round: there the delivery would grant nothing, so a human has to act.
- **What a caller does.** Treat `non_usd` as success for Stripe (it is 200) and as an alert for a person; a caller that tested `outcome === "applied"` for success must accept `non_usd` too. `warn` can page. The count of `money.skipped` events in a month is the number of deliveries the USD reports leave out.
- **Marked done?** This closes "answered 200 `applied` with no signal". It does not book anything in another currency: the roadmap item "non-USD books" stays open (an hledger commodity per currency, and the reports that would need to read them).
- **`reconcile` still shows the money.** A Stripe invoice or payout in another currency is still `missing_in_log` against Stripe's lists (there is no `payment.succeeded`); the new `skipped` count says why. Balance transactions in another currency were already ignored (`balance_transaction_other_currency`). A Stripe refund of a non-USD invoice has no `payment.refunded` to pair with and would show as `missing_in_log` too (not run).
- **Old readers.** `money.skipped` is a new event type in the envelope's enum: a reader built before it (`atm validate` of an older atm) rejects a log that holds one. The log's `v` is unchanged (a new optional field is not a new version, a new type is a choice noted here).
- **Disputes in another currency are marked too.** A `charge.dispute.created` or `.closed` whose dispute is not in USD, and that moved money (its `balance_transactions` is not empty), is answered 200 `non_usd` with `body.skipped` listing `dispute <du_...> <amount> <currency>`, writes no `payment.disputed` and no `dispute.closed`, and leaves one `money.skipped` marker of kind `dispute` (`amount` is the dispute's own, in its currency's minor units). Its idem is `stripe:<dispute id>:non_usd` and its date is the opening line's (the withdrawal's `created`, else the dispute's), so the created event, the closed event, either alone, and a re-send under another event id all leave the same one marker, and `reconcile` counts the dispute once. Each of those deliveries is still answered `non_usd` and warns again (the answer comes from what the delivery decided, before the outbox drops the duplicate marker): a closing tells the person again that the books are short for that dispute. The claim row keeps `non_usd`. The credits a lost dispute takes back are unchanged. A dispute of a payment the books never held (a non-USD invoice, or a charge that names no handled invoice) is marked too, as its refund is. An inquiry (`warning_*`, an empty `balance_transactions`) in another currency is answered `applied` with no marker: Stripe took nothing, so there is no money line to leave out (the recorded inquiry, `fixtures/billing/stripe/recorded/charge.dispute.created.json`, has an empty list), and if it becomes a chargeback its closing event carries the withdrawal and is marked then. Not checked: a dispute in one currency withdrawn from a balance in another (the balance transaction is then in the balance's currency, with an exchange rate); the marker takes the dispute's own amount and currency and does not read the balance transaction's.

## A paid invoice the handler refuses (e2e matrix finding D4)

- **The problem.** An `invoice.paid` means Stripe has collected the money. The handler could still refuse it after taking the claim: the menu does not know a line (`unmatched_line`), the invoice is for another subscription than the member's (`tier_on_other_subscription`, `addon_only_subscription`), or the grant rules or the engine throw (`rejected`). A refusal wrote nothing: the claim was released, the invoice not indexed, and no `payment.succeeded` booked. The books lacked the whole payment (260.00 income, 7.84 fee, 252.16 cash in the matrix case) until a person fixed the cause and the event was sent again; Stripe retries a 422 for up to three days and then stops. `atm reconcile` noticed, but only as `missing_in_log invoice` with the fee and net totals.
- **What is booked now.** In the transaction that releases the claim, the handler also appends the payment the invoice would book if it had been applied: the same `payment.succeeded` (idem `stripe:<invoice id>`, the fee `deps.paymentFee` gave, the income account from the menu's reading of the lines, `other` for a line it does not know), or the `money.skipped` marker for an invoice that is not in USD. The answer is unchanged: `422 refused`, with the reason, and the message now says the payment is booked. The claim is released, the invoice is not indexed, no row is written, no credit event is made. `handleStripeEvent` returns the line as `refused.payment` and leaves `events` empty and the message as the refusal's own, so a caller that does not know the field behaves as before; the sentence about the payment (and that a refund instead of a fix is entered by hand) is the reference handler's, added where it books the line.
- **Every reason an `invoice.paid` can be refused with, and what is booked.**

  | When | Answer | Booked? | Why |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | before the claim | 401 `bad_signature` | no | the event is not Stripe's |
  | before the claim | 400 `bad_request` | no | no invoice to read |
  | before the claim | 422 `livemode_mismatch` | no | a test-mode event must not reach live books, or the reverse |
  | before the claim | 422 `school_mismatch` | no | another school's money; booking it here would put it in the wrong books |
  | before the claim | 422 `missing_metadata` (half a holder, or ids that make no subject key) | no | the holder and the school are not known |
  | before the claim | 200 `ignored` (no `b4u_school` and no `family_id`) | no | another product on the same Stripe account |
  | after the claim | 422 `unknown_school` (`deps.context` returns null) | no | no menu: no time zone to date the line, and the service does not serve the school |
  | after the claim | 422 `unknown_mapping` (another API version) | no | the shapes differ, so the amount cannot be trusted |
  | after the claim | 422 `bad_event`: the body does not normalise, or has no `api_version` | no | the amount cannot be read |
  | after the claim | 422 `bad_event`: the fee the caller gave is not a whole number of cents from 0 to what was paid | no | the caller's bug; the number would be wrong |
  | after the claim | 422 `missing_metadata` from the grant rules | no | no holder (not reachable once the metadata check above has passed) |
  | after the claim | 422 `unmatched_line` | **yes** | the invoice is Stripe's, the holder is named, only the menu is behind; the line is `other` unless the menu knows another line |
  | after the claim | 422 `tier_on_other_subscription` | **yes** | the money is certain; the income is the tier's |
  | after the claim | 422 `addon_only_subscription` | **yes** | the money is certain; the income is the add-on's |
  | after the claim | 422 `rejected` (the engine rejected a command, or the grant rules threw) | **yes** | the money is certain; what failed is only the grant |
  | after the claim | 409 `stale_revision`, 409 `busy`, 200 `duplicate` | n/a | not refusals: the delivery is retried whole or already done |
  | after the claim | 500 (`paymentFee` or `context` threw, the database failed) | n/a | the claim stays; unchanged |
  | n/a for `invoice.paid` | 409 `unmatched_invoice`, 409 `unknown_subscription`, `unmatched_booking` | n/a | refunds, disputes, a cancel before an invoice, a booking session |

  Where each is made: `src/handler/stripe-webhook.ts` (`handleStripeWebhook`, `locateHolder`, `applyOnce`), `src/billing/handle.ts` (`handleStripeEvent`, `decide`, `PAID_INVOICE_REFUSALS`). The classification is the code's, tested one reason at a time in `tests/handler-refused-payment.test.ts`.
- **The retry after the fix.** The menu is fixed, Stripe's next retry (or the owner's re-send) is applied: the credits are granted, the invoice is indexed, the claim is `done`. That delivery makes the same `payment.succeeded` and the outbox keeps the first row of an idem, so the income is booked once. The fee and the income account are the ones of the first delivery, as they already were for any invoice (a fee that was unknown at first is not booked later; a line the menu did not know stays `other` rather than moving to its new account). A month boundary changes nothing: the line is dated by the invoice's `paid_at` in the school's time zone, in both deliveries. A non-USD invoice leaves the `money.skipped` marker at the refusal and none more at the retry, which is answered `200 non_usd` as always.
- **Refunds, disputes and `invoice_payment.paid` of a refused invoice are unchanged: they wait.** The invoice is not indexed, so its `invoice_payment.paid` answers 409 `unmatched_invoice` and a refund or dispute 409 `unmatched_payment`, retried by Stripe, until the invoice is applied. Until then reconcile shows `missing_in_log refund` (before this change it showed the invoice and the fee as well). After the fix and the retry, the link and the refund apply as for any invoice, and the refund takes the credits back. Books after a refused-and-booked invoice that stays refused for good (a second subscription for a member whose first ended; `tier_on_other_subscription` when the member is refunded instead of the cause fixed): the income stays booked and a refund is not; it has to be entered by hand.
- **Options rejected.**
  - *Book the payment before the grant decision, for every refusal* (the first idea in the matrix). It would book events whose amount or holder cannot be trusted (`unknown_mapping`, a body that does not normalise, a fee that is wrong) and the pre-claim refusals above.
  - *A label or payload marker on the payment that says the credits were refused.* The payload schema would allow it, but the outbox keeps the first row of an idem, so the marker would still be on the line after the credits were granted: a statement that is false from then on. The 422 and its message are the alarm; nothing in the books says "refused".
  - *A new event type for "credits refused".* A reader built before the type rejects a log that holds it (the lesson of `money.skipped`), and nothing would read it.
  - *Record the invoice as decided with no credits and answer 200.* The old `addon_only_subscription` did this and charged the member with no error anywhere. It would also make a refund work, but the alarm is the point, and a re-send could never grant.
  - *Index the refused invoice so its refund and dispute find it, and book their money lines with `refused.payment` too* ("option E"). A refund of an invoice with no grants row has no holder to name (`decideAndRun` returns nothing for a refund when the subject has no rows, and the webhook would answer 200 and lose the refund), needs a new input for "paid but not applied", and must refuse even a partial refund (otherwise a refund of an unapplied invoice is answered 200 with no line, for good). That is a larger change on the refund and dispute paths for a rarer case. It is the follow-up if refused-for-good invoices turn out to be common.
  - *Count the 422s in `outboxStatus`.* Useful, but it leaves the books short.
- **Callers other than the reference handler.** `handleStripeEvent` returns `refused.payment` for these four reasons. A family service with its own handler that wants the books right appends those events to its outbox in the transaction where it releases the claim (`appendToOutbox`); one that ignores the field is exactly as before.

## The family service's own commands in the outbox (#46)

## The family service does not call the handler, the shipper or the recorders

- [ ] **The family service does not call atm's handler, the shipper or the new recorders.** atm's side is done (#47, `a9479d7d3`, on `dev`, not released): `atm ship` exits 0 done, 1 a conflict, 2 any other error, with `--json`; `outboxStatus` for a lag alert; `atm report`; the "Running it" recipe in the README and an adoption section in `UPGRADING.md`. Moving off its own grant rules (`school-billing/grant.ts`) is `buzz-school` work and open. The recipe's claims about `buzz-school`'s runtime were read, not run, and these were not verified: that Stripe retries on 409 and stops on 422 and 200, that `scheduleJob` accepts the README's snippet, that the handler's DDL passes its migration check under Litestream, the cron one-liner (it leaves `alert` and `$SCHOOL` undefined), and that hledger can be installed in its image (the recipe runs `atm report` off the machine).

## Reconciliation against Stripe (#48) and its known mismatch

- **A reconciliation against Stripe** (#48, `f76c1a21c`, on `dev`, not released): `reconcile` and `atm reconcile --root --school --month --stripe <dir>` compare paid invoices, refunds, disputes, payouts, fees and the net with Stripe's list output; exit 0 clean, 1 differences, 2 bad input (a missing Stripe file exits 2). It compares money, not credit balances or grants. The review of #48 fixed seven bugs, among them a missing file passing silently.
  - [ ] **Known mismatch, found by running the handler from #49 and reading its ids (not by running reconcile on it):** a handler-made `payment.refunded` carries the Stripe EVENT id (`payload.id` is `evt_…`, `idem` is `stripe:evt_…`), while `reconcile` matches refunds on the refund id (`re_…`). On a handler-made log every refund would show as a difference. Fix: the refund event should carry the charge or invoice id (and the refund id when Stripe gives one), and `reconcile` should match on it. Invoices (`in_…`) and payouts (`po_…`) match.
  - [ ] The `stripe` CLI commands in the README were not run; `--stripe-account` on `list` and some resource names are unconfirmed.

## Revenue timing

- [ ] **Revenue is booked when the invoice is paid.** A yearly plan's 12 months land as income at once and there is no deferred-revenue account, so the income statement is cash-basis. Credits are counted in units, not dollars, so the balance sheet shows no liability for unused credits. Owner decision: cash basis is enough, or book deferred revenue (and a dollar value for credits).
